Experience the Ultimate Muslim Youth Summer Camp Adventure!

May 8, 2024 By Halal Fun Club 1 Min Read

Engaging, enriching environment

Join us at Halal Fun Club's summer camp for an unforgettable experience filled with adventure, spiritual growth, and lasting friendships. Our camp is designed to provide a safe and engaging environment for Muslim youth aged 13 to 19. Don't miss out on this opportunity to create memories that will last a lifetime!

"Camping in nature helps youth disconnect from electronic screens, stargazing reminds them of the Creator, and cooperation in camp tasks builds their leadership."

Over the course of a week, campers will engage in hikes, canoeing, archery, target simulated hunting, campfire talks, stargazing, and regular congregational prayers. We ensure a positive environment where youth build character, friendship, and strong community values.
