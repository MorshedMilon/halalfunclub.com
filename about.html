Who We Are

Halal Fun Club Summer Camp & Outdoor Adventures

Our Mission

We some outdoor enthusiasts, from different ethnic and professional backgrounds, are putting efforts to make outdoor sports more available to our communities. Having internet readily available to all age, people including our youth are spending more and more time on screen and therefore reducing connection with the nature. Going outdoor give us the opportunity to connect with the nature, to appreciate the creation of the Almighty God.

Building Team & Leadership Skills

We go camping in the great Canadian nature, where we forget our daily busy schedule and enjoy hiking, bird watching, and exploring wildness. We go fishing, swimming, boating in the lakes. We of course go hunting, shooting arrows, balloons. We enjoy doing BBQ together, storytelling while lighting up campfire and when night falls, we witness the magnificent sky full of stars. While stargazing, sometimes we capture the close up view of the entire moon through a telescope.

All these activities help us build team spirit. It strengthens our bonding as a family, as friends and help us build leadership skills through cooperation. It refreshes our minds and bodies and helps us engage more in our day to day activities and help us perform even better.

Spiritual Connection

While enjoying outdoor sports, we never forget our prayers. We continue praying together and remain thankful for what we are blessed with. Come join us in our outdoor adventures. Let's replace the negative elements of our lives with positive energy and spirit. Let's become better human and prepare to serve our communities and our great country.

Why Families Choose Us:

  • 100% Halal Environment
  • Congregation Prayers and Reminders
  • Professional Mentors & Safety First
  • Engaging Youth Activities (13-19 years)
