Enriching Summer Camp Experience for Muslim Youth

May 8, 2024 By Halal Fun Club 1 Min Read

Engaging, Enriching, Adventure

Join our Halal Fun Club summer camp for Muslim youth aged 13 to 19. Experience adventure, spiritual growth, and lasting friendships in a safe and engaging environment. Develop new skills, create unforgettable memories, and build a strong community.

"Our summer camp sessions focus on replacing negative elements in everyday lives with positive energy, brotherly spirit, cooperation, and love for nature."

We invite parents to register their teenagers for this healthy summer retreat. Through hikes, sports, stargazing, team tasks, and praying together, our camp builds leadership skills and reinforces positive Islamic values in an action-packed week.
