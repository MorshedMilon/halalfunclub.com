Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have compiled the answers for you

Our camp runs from 29th July to 4th August. Detailed schedule, directions, and maps will be provided upon registration.
Our camp welcomes youth aged 13 to 19, providing them with enriching and adventurous experiences. Additionally, we also open our doors to families, ensuring that everyone can enjoy the activities and create lasting memories together.
Our price is very reasonable. Only $400/- including Food, Drink and Transportation / Motorhome.
A detailed packing list will be provided after registration, including recommended clothing, sleeping bag, personal toiletries, mosquito repellent, and any specific gear needed for outdoor activities.
You can reach us by calling 647-803-0806, sending an email to halalfunclub@gmail.com, or using our Contact Form. We are always happy to answer your questions and provide support!

Do not hesitate. Our spots are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis.
